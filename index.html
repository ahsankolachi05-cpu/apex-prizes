Good to know

Questions, answered

Is Spot the Ball a game of skill?

Yes. The ball is removed from a genuine match photograph and you judge where its centre was, using the players' eye lines and body positions. After each competition closes, an independent panel of expert judges decides the winning position — the entry closest to it wins the prize. It is a competition of skill, not a lottery.

When are competitions drawn, and are they guaranteed?

Every competition closes at the date and time shown on its card and is judged within 48 hours — guaranteed, regardless of how many tickets have sold. There are no extensions and no rollovers.

Can I take cash instead of the car?

Yes — every car competition carries a tax-free cash alternative, shown on its card. If you win, simply choose the car or the cash when we call you. Cash prizes are paid by bank transfer within 5 working days.

How do I pay, and is it secure?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Payments are processed over an encrypted connection by a PCI DSS Level 1 provider with 3-D Secure 2 verification — your full card number never reaches our servers.

Who can enter?

Entrants must be 18 or over and resident in the United Kingdom. A free postal entry route is available for every competition — see the terms for the address and format. Please play responsibly: only ever spend what you can afford.

How will I know if I've won?

We phone the winner within 48 hours of judging and follow up by email. Winners are also announced on this page and our social channels. If we can't reach a winner within 14 days, the prize goes to the next-closest entry.
